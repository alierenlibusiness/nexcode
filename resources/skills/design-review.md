# Design Review

## When to use

Reviewing a proposed approach.

## Checklist

- Check the problem statement is real and agreed before critiquing the solution.
- Evaluate against the stated constraints, not your personal preference.
- Ask what happens at 10x scale and at failure.
- Identify the one-way doors — decisions that will be expensive to reverse.
- Propose a concrete alternative when you reject an approach.

## Verification

- Every objection names the constraint it violates.
