# Responsive Design

## When to use

Any layout work.

## Checklist

- Design the narrow layout first; widening is easier than cramming.
- Use fluid units and intrinsic sizing before reaching for breakpoints.
- Let content decide breakpoints, not device names.
- Give wide content (tables, code, diagrams) its own horizontal scroll container.
- Ensure touch targets are at least 44px and not crowded.

## Verification

- Check 320px, 768px, 1024px and 1440px.
- Confirm the page body never scrolls horizontally.
