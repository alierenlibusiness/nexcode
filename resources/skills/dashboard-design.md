# Dashboard Design

## When to use

Building a monitoring or status view.

## Checklist

- Answer the single most important question in the top-left.
- Group related metrics; do not scatter one story across the page.
- Show trend and threshold, not just the current number.
- Use colour for state, and pair it with a second cue for accessibility.
- Keep refresh predictable and show when the data was last updated.

## Verification

- Ask someone what the system's state is after five seconds of looking.
